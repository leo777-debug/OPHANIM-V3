# Customer training consent register

No customers or customer documents are approved for training. Company uploads
are RAG only and never consumed by the synthetic generator or training script.

Future entries must record organization, document hashes, written consent proof
location, authorized purpose, expiry/revocation, approving person and date,
redaction reviewer, redacted-output hashes and deletion obligations. Keep the
actual proof and personal data in private company storage, never this repository
or Kaggle. Redact names, emails, addresses, phone numbers, identifiers and rare
shipment combinations before any separately approved training use.
